Tangut · Tibetan transcription

Tangut Tibetan Transcription Scheme

A scheme for spelling Tangut in Tibetan script, based on Gong Xun’s reconstruction, with an emphasis on simplicity and natural Tibetan shaping. This scheme is recreational.

Kelvin YangAugust 2026
01

Initials

The scheme does not distinguish the tś/tṣ or k/q initial sets. The latter are conditioned uvularized allophones and remain recoverable from the rhyme grade.

02

Vowels

The scheme marks Grades I, II, III, and IV respectively with postposed འ, postposed ཨ, zero, and postposed ཡ. Grade IV comprises R.3, R.11, R.20, R.31, R.37, and R.47; a genuine y medial remains subjoined ྱ.

GX vw is the conditioned Grade-I/II realization after v; its w is not written separately.

Although based on GX, the scheme also aims to reflect traditional rhyme-table categories. GX merges R.92/R.100 and R.79/R.101, but this orthography adds preposed འ in R.100 and R.101 to preserve their traditional distinction.
