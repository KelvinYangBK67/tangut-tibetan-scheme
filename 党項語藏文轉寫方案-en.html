Tangut · Tibetan transcription

Tangut Tibetan Transcription Scheme

This recreational scheme spells Tangut in Tibetan script. It is based on GX202411 and aims at a compact, natural Tibetan orthography.

Initials

Each GX initial is assigned a Tibetan nominal letter; conditioned GX pairs such as tś/tṣ and k/q share one spelling because their distribution is recoverable from the rhyme grade. Derived f, lh, ll, gh use ༹; complex stacks place all subjoined letters before ༹ for shaping stability.

Although the scheme is based on GX, it also aims to preserve traditional rhyme-table categories. GX merges R.92 with R.100 and R.79 with R.101; this orthography therefore adds preposed འ- to R.100 and R.101. Automatic GX conversion defaults to unmarked R.92/R.79. Prefix the vowel nucleus with a backslash—e.g. rtś\ər¹ or rts\er¹—to request the marked spelling.

Tests and full rhyme inventory

The Chinese web edition contains the full word, sentence, and paragraph tests, and its searchable rhyme table lists R.1–105. The symbols and transcriptions in that inventory are language-independent; question marks belong to uncertain GX reconstructions and are intentionally omitted from the Tibetan column.
