This scheme spells Tangut in Tibetan script. It is based on \href{https://semakosa.github.io/tangut-pronunciation-db/docs/GX202411-zh.html}{Gong Xun’s reconstruction} and aims at simple, natural Tibetan orthography. The scheme is recreational.

Although based on GX, the scheme also aims to reflect traditional rhyme-table categories. GX merges R.92/R.100 and R.79/R.101, but this orthography adds preposed འ in R.100 and R.101 to preserve their traditional distinction.
